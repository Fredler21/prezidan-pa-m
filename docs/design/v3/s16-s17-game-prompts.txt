PRODUCT: « Prezidan Pa M », a neutral Haitian civic website for the 2027 presidential election. UI language: Haitian Kreyòl, copy exactly as written. Its main subject is now the civic game « Si m te prezidan » (If I were president): the visitor plays a 5 year term, makes 8 hard decisions about Haiti today, sees the consequences, and comes out understanding why their real vote matters. [[Mobile viewport 390px wide.||Desktop viewport 1280px wide, content max-width 1160px centered, 12 column grid, 24px gutters.]]

ART DIRECTION: the work of a senior product designer at a top studio. Premium, calm, presidential, never childish, never "gamey" (no cartoons, no confetti graphics, no badges with stars, no 3D, no illustrations of people). Editorial typography does the work: very large tight headlines, strong hierarchy, generous white space, crisp 1px hairlines, precise 8pt spacing, one clear primary action per view. The page background is pure white #FFFFFF; the game "Eta peyi a" (state of the country) panel is the only dark element on screen, solid night indigo #0B1230 with white text, like a presidential briefing card.

TOKENS: ink #0B1A4A, muted #5B6480, hairline #E1E5F0, soft fill #F3F5FA, ultramarine #1A3BB5 (primary actions, selected states, bars, links), ultramarine tint #EAEEFA (icon wells, selected option background), night indigo #0B1230 (the country panel and dark band only), on dark: text #FFFFFF and secondary #B9C4E6, panel inner rows #141D45 with 1px rgba(255,255,255,.10) border. Status colors used only for small numbers and chips: up #15803D on #E7F6EC, down #D21034 on #FDECEF. Bricolage Grotesque for everything (<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bricolage+Grotesque:opsz,wght@12..96,200..800&display=swap">), tabular numbers for every figure, Material Symbols Outlined weight 300 for icons. Sentence case, no uppercase labels, no letter spacing tricks. Radii: cards 24px, inner tiles 16px, buttons and chips 999px. Light cards: white, 1px #E1E5F0 border, shadow 0 1px 2px rgba(11,26,74,.04), 0 12px 32px rgba(11,26,74,.06). Header: white, 72px, 1px hairline bottom, the logo image at left (a glossy navy serif wordmark « PREZIDAN » with a red brush stroke I, a small bust in a tricorn hat above, a small red ballot box « PAM » under it, about 48px tall), [[the « Kreyòl ▾ » pill and a round initial button « M » at right||nav « Akèy » « Priyorite » « Kandida » « Rezilta » « Sondaj » « Jwèt », the « Kreyòl ▾ » pill, a moon icon button and a round account button with « M » « Marie ▾ » at right]]. Motion described in CSS: cards rise 16px and fade in, meter bars slide to their new value, numbers count, the selected option scales 1.02; respect prefers-reduced-motion. No dashes in any text. Neutral: no party colors, no candidate is ever recommended.
